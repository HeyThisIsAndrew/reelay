# The 60-second demo: shot list

One finished video in, a complete publishing package out. Film it on the Mac in the Black theme with the
**Hey This Is Andrew** profile. Screen at 1440 × 900, cursor highlighting on, system notifications off.

**Before you roll**

- Export a finished Short from Resolve with **File → Export → Subtitle** next to it, same name:
  `Why I bought the 16-35 GM twice.mp4` and `Why I bought the 16-35 GM twice.srt`, on the Desktop.
- Reelay open on the Library, the Board's first column with a few tickets, YouTube connected (Advanced
  Setup), Settings → Brand voice showing "Hey, This Is Andrew ✓ in use".
- Do one Repurpose run first so the model is warm; drafts take about a minute, so shot 4 is cut.

| # | Time | Shot | On screen | Voice-over (or caption) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00 to 0:05 | Desktop, Reelay window beside the Finder | The .mp4 and .srt on the Desktop | "This is a finished video. Getting it onto six platforms is the hard part." |
| 2 | 0:05 to 0:12 | Drag the .mp4 onto Reelay | It lands on the Board, the ticket opens: cover, player, a laptop badge "Made on this computer", the transcript filled from the .srt | "Drop it in. Reelay makes a ticket, with the transcript from my caption file." |
| 3 | 0:12 to 0:18 | Click **Repurpose with Muse Spark** | The dialog: the source panel shows the transcript and the voice "Hey, This Is Andrew"; tick Allow Muse Spark; **Generate Proposal** | "It drafts every platform from what I actually say, in my voice." |
| 4 | 0:18 to 0:32 | (cut the wait) The proposal | YouTube title, description with my footer, TikTok, Instagram, Bluesky, Threads, Facebook, each with its live count against the limit | "Title, description, captions, all within each platform's rules." |
| 5 | 0:32 to 0:38 | Edit one word, **Apply to Ticket** | The caption boxes fill in on the ticket | "I review it, change what I want, and apply." |
| 6 | 0:38 to 0:45 | The readiness ring | The ring goes green, "Ready" on each platform row; Posting to chips ticked | "Readiness checks the file and every caption. It's green." |
| 7 | 0:45 to 0:53 | **Publish Now** to YouTube (or Schedule) | The YouTube row moves to Scheduled; coverage dots update on the card | "YouTube goes out from here." |
| 8 | 0:53 to 0:58 | TikTok ⋯ → **Prepare to Publish** | Caption copied, TikTok's uploader opens, the .mp4 selected in Finder | "TikTok and Instagram get everything ready to paste." |
| 9 | 0:58 to 1:00 | End card | "Reelay. Get everything ready to publish everywhere." | (no voice) |

**Honesty notes for the edit**

- Don't show Bluesky, Threads or Facebook posting unless those accounts are connected and automatic posting is
  on in Settings. Their rows read "posted by hand" otherwise, which is fine to show.
- YouTube uploads from an unaudited Google project are private: say "goes out" rather than "goes public", or
  show the scheduled state.
- Keep the real wait out of the cut, but don't speed up the readiness ring: it changes the moment copy is
  applied.

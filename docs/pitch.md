# Reelay: one page

**What it is.** A Mac app that turns one finished video into a complete, platform-ready publishing package.
Drop in the export from your editor and Reelay builds everything each platform needs: a YouTube title,
description and tags, and the TikTok, Instagram, Bluesky, Threads and Facebook captions. Drafts are written
from the video's transcript in your own voice profile and checked against each platform's limits. A readiness
check turns green when it can go. Reelay posts to YouTube itself, and to Bluesky, Threads and Facebook when
you turn them on; it hands TikTok and Instagram to you ready to paste, with the caption copied and the file
selected.

**Who it's for.** A creator producing enough video that cross-posting has become administrative work: five
or more videos a week across three or more platforms, usually one person or a small team, who has noticed that
the hard part isn't picking a time to post, it's rewriting the same video six ways and remembering what went
where.

**The price.** $79, one time, with 12 months of updates included. No subscription, no seats, no Reelay
account. Your library and database stay on your Mac. Repurpose uses your own Meta API key, so AI drafting is
billed by Meta at cost with nothing added.

**Why it isn't Buffer.** Buffer schedules posts you've already written; Reelay writes and checks the posts
from the video itself, so the work that's left is reviewing, not rewriting.

**What's true today (1.0).** Built and used on the founder's own channel: 405 of his videos in the Library,
real drafts on his real Shorts that needed no rewriting beyond one line. Posting to YouTube, Bluesky, Threads
and Facebook needs each creator's own account connected; one-click sign-in for TikTok and Meta waits on
those platforms' app reviews, and TikTok and Instagram stay copy-and-paste until their posting APIs approve
Reelay.
